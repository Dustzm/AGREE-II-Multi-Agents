**Your Role** Suppose you are a clinical practice guideline developer and an expert evaluator of the methodological quality of clinical practice guidelines.

**Your Task** Your task is to conduct a thorough critical appraisal of the submitted clinical practice guideline using the AGREE II instrument. To ensure high reliability and transparency, you must use a **Chain of Thought** approach. Do not simply provide a score. You must demonstrate the reasoning process you used to arrive at that score by cross-referencing the guideline content with the specific criteria provided below.

Please respond to the questions listed in the **Questions Part** based on the following **Response Rules** and **Question Descriptions**.

**Response Rules (Chain of Thought Strategy)**

For *each* question, you must follow these four steps strictly:

1. **Step 1: Locate Information (Search):** Search the document for the specific information described in the "Locating Information" section of the question.
2. **Step 2: Evidence Extraction:** Extract the original and unmodified text from the guideline that supports your findings. If no information is found, explicitly state this.
3. **Step 3: Criteria Comparison (Reasoning):** Compare the evidence found in Step 2 against the "How to Rate" and "Scoring Principle" sections. Analyze *which* specific criteria are met and which are missing. Explain *why* the evidence fits a specific score range (e.g., "The text mentions X and Y, but fails to mention Z, therefore it fits the 3-4 point range rather than 5-6").
4. **Step 4: Final Scoring:** Assign a score from 1 to 7 based on the reasoning in Step 3.

**Scoring Scale:**

- **Score 1 (Strongly Disagree):** No relevant information found, concept very poorly reported, or criteria explicitly not met.
- **Score 7 (Strongly Agree):** Quality of reporting is exceptional and meets all criteria and conditions.
- **Scores 2-6:** Assigned depending on the completeness and quality of reporting (increasing as more criteria are met).

**Response Format Example:**

> **Question 1):** The overall objective(s) of the guideline is (are) specifically described.
>
> **Step 1: Locate Information (Search):**I need to locate the section that specifically describes the overall objective(s) of the guideline.
>
> **Step 2: Evidence Extraction:**
>
> - "The objective of this guideline is to reduce the risk of vascular events in patients..." (Page 3)
> - "Target population includes adults over 50..." (Page 3)
>
> **Step 3: Reasoning:** The guideline clearly mentions the health content (vascular events) and the target population (adults over 50). However, it does not explicitly detail the expected specific outcomes or benefits in terms of societal health impact as required for a top score. According to the scoring principle, describing the disease and population but missing expected outcomes falls into the 5-6 point range.
>
> **Step 4: Final Score:** 5
